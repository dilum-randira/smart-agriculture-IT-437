# Microclimate reference photographs

These are illustrative reference images, not evidence of our field, cultivar,
C1 hardware, sensor deployment or research validation. Retrieved October 21,
2026 from the primary Wikimedia Commons file pages below. Local versions are
EXIF-oriented, resized to fit 480/960-pixel bounds, converted to RGB and WebP
(quality 80); the UI additionally crops with `object-fit: cover`. No synthetic
devices or fields have been composited into them.

The botanical image is identified as **Capsicum chinense** by its source. It is
not independently verified as a Sri Lankan Nai Miris cultivar. The irrigation
photograph depicts **cherry tomatoes and wetted soil**, not Nai Miris. The weather
station is a third-party reference station, not the project's C1 prototype.

Attribution, license links, adaptation notes and cultivar limitations are also
visible on the dashboard. Asset URLs are bundled locally by Vite, so runtime
rendering needs no Wikimedia connection. External links are for source/license
reference only. The 3D scene and static diagram are original conceptual geometry,
not photographs or representations of an installed field.
